Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we handle your information.

Welcome to MA Academy's Privacy Policy. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how it may be used, and how we respect your privacy. By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use MA Academy, you may voluntarily provide certain information, such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Information included in messages you send us.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your questions and messages.
  • Communicate with you about MA Academy.
  • Improve our educational content and services.
  • Maintain and improve the website.

3. Contact Form

When you submit the contact form, the website opens WhatsApp with your message prepared for sending. You choose whether to send it through WhatsApp. The website itself does not automatically send or store the message.

4. Cookies and Tracking

MA Academy does not currently claim to use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes. However, hosting providers or external services may process technical information when you visit the website.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may include links to external services such as WhatsApp, Instagram, GitHub, and email providers. When you visit these services, their own privacy policies and terms apply. MA Academy does not control how these third parties process your information.

6. Data Protection

We aim to respect your privacy and take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us. However, no website or method of internet transmission can guarantee complete security.

7. Children's Privacy

MA Academy is an educational website. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by applicable law.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion of information you have shared with us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page. We encourage you to review it regularly.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
